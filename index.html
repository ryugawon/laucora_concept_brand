OUR STORY

THE COURAGE
TO CHOOSE.

LAUCORA carries forward the instinct behind SAYBONS: question the default, then make another choice genuinely accessible. This time, the question is not only how we eat — but how we use our time.

YOUR PACE?

The city will keep moving. That does not mean every person has to move in exactly the same way. LAUCORA gives people a place to decide when to move, when to pause and how long a moment should last.

FROM SAYBONS TO LAUCORA

A NEW CHAPTER,
ROOTED IN THE
SAME COURAGE.

Daphane Loke's path into F&B and the creation of SAYBONS reflects a consistent attitude: familiar conventions can be questioned, redesigned and made easier for more people to enter. LAUCORA translates that attitude into a new café culture built around personal pace.

CONVENTION → QUESTION → NEW CHOICE
SAYBONS changed how people could experience French food.
LAUCORA asks how people want to experience their time.

LOCATIONS

FIND
YOUR PACE.

No invented street address is needed for the concept. The spatial idea starts in Singapore: one café, multiple speeds, with every zone supporting a different use of time.

STARTING CITY
SINGAPORE

A dense, fast-moving city is the right place to prove the idea: a café does not have to force one pace. LAUCORA can serve a three-minute stop, a focused hour or an unplanned afternoon.

GO ZONE

Mobile order, quick pickup, grab-and-go food and clear circulation.

SOLO ZONE

Single seats with natural light, power access and enough privacy to feel unobserved.

STAY ZONE

Longer tables and softer seating for work, conversation and slower café food.

ORA PLUSH KEYRING

Always on my time.

A soft Open-C clock with short limbs, a calm face and one tiny coffee in hand. The expression stays understated so the character still belongs to LAUCORA's urban world.

LAU PLUSH KEYRING

Growing at my own pace.

A long laurel leaf silhouette with a white center vein, tiny limbs and a restrained embroidered expression. ORA is time; LAU is growth.
